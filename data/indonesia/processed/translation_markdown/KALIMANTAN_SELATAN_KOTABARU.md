# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms mean:

- 1. Region is the Regency of Kotabaru.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government Administration.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Kotabaru.
- 4. Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the Regional People's Representative Council of Kotabaru Regency.
- 5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit within the scope of the Kotabaru Regency Government.
- 6. Child is a person who is not yet 18 years old, including a child still in the womb.
- 7. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
- 8. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
- 9. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence that are carried out by the Integrated Service Center.
- 10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
- 11. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.
- 12. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of distrust or severe psychological suffering in a person.
- 13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with other persons for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
- 14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
- 15. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
- 16. Service is activities and immediate actions carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
- 17. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
- 18. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
- 19. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
- 20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
- 21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
- 22. Institution is an agency/service/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.
- 23. Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of empowerment for Women and Children victims of violence.
- 24. Regional Action Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RAD, is the foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical institutions, and the community, in carrying out prevention and protection activities for women and children victims of violence.
- 25. Safe House is temporary housing, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
- 26. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 27. Family is persons who have blood relations in a direct line upward or downward and in a collateral line up to the third degree, or who have marital relations, or persons who are dependents of the woman and/or child.
- 28. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
- 29. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as APBD, is the annual financial plan of the Regional Government that is discussed and jointly approved by the Regional Government and DPRD and stipulated by Regional Regulation.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

(1) The implementation of protection for women and children is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for the rights of women and children;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination in the empowerment of women and children;
- d. the best interests of women and children; and
- e. restoration of social and economic rights for women and children who experience acts of violence or neglect.
  - (2) The objectives of implementing protection for women and children include:
- a. prevent acts of violence or neglect against women and children including trafficking in persons;
- b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
- c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- d. provide services to women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
- e. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and
- f. seek to obtain compensation/restitution for losses suffered by victims from perpetrators of violence.

# CHAPTER III

SCOPE

#### Article 3

(1) Protection of women and children in the region includes prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons, and domestic neglect including child deviant behavior.

(2) The implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence is the joint obligation and responsibility of the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.

(3) In the region, P2TP2A may be formed which is the result of joint initiative between the Regional Government and non-governmental parties.

# CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

(1) Every woman and child who becomes a victim of physical violence, exploitation or human trafficking has the right to obtain protection and services.

(2) Protection and services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- a. complaints or requests for protection from the person concerned;
- b. confidential handling or placement of victims in safe locations and houses whose existence is kept secret to avoid intimidation and threats;
- c. medical services/health treatment in the form of care and recovery from injuries or physical conditions of victims by medical and paramedical personnel;
- d. medicolegal services for evidence in the legal field;
- e. protection by law enforcement officials;
- f. provision of information regarding legal rights and obligations to victims or guardians of victims as well as identification of incidents;
- g. continuous handling up to the Rehabilitation stage;
- h. psychological assistance to restore the traumatic condition of victims and restore victims' self-confidence;
- i. assistance support for legal processes and obtaining the right to damages or compensation;
- j. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member (social reintegration); and
- k. economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that victims can become independent.
  - (3) In the case that the victim is a child, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in paragraph (2), also obtains special rights including:
- a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigma;
- e. the right to obtain freedom; and
- f. the right to obtain compensation from the perpetrator.

# CHAPTER V

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 5

The obligations and responsibilities in providing protection for women and children from acts of violence are the joint responsibility of:

- a. regional government;
- b. society;
- c. family; and
- d. parents.

#### Article 6

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 5 letter a, include:

- a. implementing the obligations of protection for women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
- b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;
- d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;
- e. providing shelters for women and children victims of violence;
- f. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and
- g. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 7

(1) the obligations and responsibilities of society as referred to in Article 5 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
- b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
- c. participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence.
  - (3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 8

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER VI

INSTITUTIONS

## Part One

Center for the Protection of Women and Children

#### Article 9

The Regional Government is required to establish P2TP2A.

#### Article 10

(1) The Regional Government is required to facilitate the establishment of P2TP2A in the region.

(2) P2TP2A is established on the joint initiative of the Regional Government, NGOs, Universities, professional organizations, organizations and society.

(3) P2TP2A in the region is named P2TP2A Kabupaten Kotabaru.

(4) The Establishment and Working Procedures of P2TP2A in the region are stipulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Organizational Structure of P2TP2A in the Region

#### Article 11

(1) The organizational structure of P2TP2A consists of:

- a. Protector/Supervisor;
- b. Advisor;
- c. Coordinator;
- d. Chairperson;
- e. Vice Chairperson
- f. Secretary;
- g. Vice Secretary;
- h. Treasurer; and
- i. Members.
  - (2) Coordinators consist of:
- a. Division of Assistance and Advocacy;
- b. Division of Services and Recovery;
- c. Division of Network Strengthening and Information;
- d. Division of Education and Training;
- e. Case Manager; and
- f. Expert Team.
  - (3) Further provisions regarding the organizational structure of P2TP2A and the description of coordinator duties are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 12

P2TP2A builds functional networks with: a. Police;

b. Prosecutor's Office;

c. Courts;

d. Hospitals/Community Health Centers;

e. Psychiatrists; and

f. Other institutions that have similar objectives.

#### Article 13

P2TP2A as an independent institution in the region must be in one coordination with SKPD whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of Women's Empowerment and Regional Child Protection.

## Part Three

Financing of P2TP2A

#### Article 14

(1) The Government assists the operation of P2TP2A by budgeting in the APBD in accordance with the capacity and financial condition of the region.

(2) Budgeting as referred to in paragraph (1) through SKPD whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of Women's Empowerment and Regional Child Protection.

(3) For administrative order and financial use, P2TP2A is required to make work programs and accountability for the use of regional finances.

#### Article 15

(1) Financing for P2TP2A in addition to being obtained from APBD funds can be through obtaining assistance receipts from individuals, bodies, or official institutions whose existence is legally recognized.

(2) P2TP2A is required to submit annual reports for performance and finances obtained from the APBD to the Regent through SKPD whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of Women's Empowerment and Regional Child Protection.

# CHAPTER VII

PREVENTION, SERVICES AND EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

General

#### Article 16

The Regional Government through regional P2TP2A, carries out prevention and service efforts for victims of violent treatment, exploitation and neglect of Children and Women up to the empowerment of victims to be able to return to living their lives according to the dignity they possess.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence, Exploitation and Neglect of Women and Children

#### Article 17

(1) Forms of prevention as referred to in Article 16 shall be carried out through:

- a. socialization activities on laws and regulations related to the protection of Children and Women to the wider community in the region;
- b. dissemination activities of the existence of P2TP2A to be known by everyone in the region through the distribution of brochures, leaflets, pamphlets or notice boards so that incidents of violence, exploitation and neglect can be reported as soon as possible and can be handled before reaching the most severe condition;
- c. training for members who are able to disseminate norms of prohibition and sanctions to the community;
- d. opening of public complaint channels by the Regional Government or P2TP2A in every area through community networks;
- e. strengthening coordination channels with the Sub-district, Urban Village/Village Government levels;
- f. immediate handling of public complaints in all areas of the region and securing the subject of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons or neglect; and/or
- g. counseling/socialization in the form of awareness-raising of the wider community to the smallest community groups (families) especially parents to protect women and children.
  - (2) The Regional Government through P2TP2A shall conduct data collection on areas of violence based on the incidence rate as well as conduct research and studies on the vulnerability level of areas to find solutions for reduction to cessation of acts of violence, exploitation and neglect in vulnerable areas.

## Part Three

Services for Women and Children Who Experience Acts of Violence, Exploitation and Neglect

#### Article 18

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons or neglect shall be implemented in accordance with the rights of victims as referred to in Article 4 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3).

(2) To support the operations of P2TP2A, the Regent shall order the SKPD related to services as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government.

#### Article 19

The Regional Government or P2TP2A shall establish operational standards for Assistance to victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking or neglect in every stage of fulfilling the rights of victims.

#### Article 20

The provision of services and assistance to victims shall be carried out with the principles of:

- a. non-discrimination;
- b. fast, safe, and empathetic;
- c. coordination between government agencies;
- d. guarantee of confidentiality;
- e. easily accessible; and
- f. free of charge.

## Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 21

(1) The Regional Government through P2TP2A shall have the obligation to conduct economic empowerment for victims of acts of violence, exploitation and/or trafficking or neglect.

(2) Forms of empowerment for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. facilitating the necessary needs for entrepreneurship training for victims of acts of violence, in order to improve knowledge, attitude, and business skills;
- b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
- c. providing assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;
- d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large companies, as well as financial institutions to develop the businesses of victims of acts of violence;
- e. endeavoring to provide capital for victims of acts of violence; and
- f. expanding access to information and promoting the products of victims of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VIII

COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 22

(1) Cooperation is carried out to strengthen the role of P2TP2A in achieving the objectives of the protection of women and children in the regional area.

(2) Cooperation is carried out with:

- a. the government;
- b. provincial government;
- c. other regency/city governments;
- d. non-governmental organizations; and
- e. international institutions whose existence is recognized.
  - (3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2), includes:
- a. exchange of data and information;
- b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;
- c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
- d. provision of evidence and witnesses, and followed up based on the provisions of laws and regulations.
  - (4) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of a joint agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 23

(1) The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business community for support in the provision of protection for women and children in the regional area.

(2) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out through:

- a. notification of job opportunity information for women victims of acts of violence;
- b. education and training for women victims of acts of violence;
- c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and
- d. fostering and improving economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.
  - (3) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of an agreement.

# CHAPTER IX

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 24

(1) In providing protection for women and children, the community may:

- a. establish family partners at the urban village/village level by the community;
- b. establish women and children protection units within community organizations;
- c. conduct socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
- d. provide first aid to victims; and
- e. report to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
  - (2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER X

REGIONAL ACTION PLAN

#### Article 25

(1) In order to carry out obligations and responsibilities, the regional government shall establish programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) RAD as the basis for implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(2) RAD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).

(3) Further provisions regarding RAD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 26

(1) The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the provision of protection for women and children from acts of violence.

(2) The Regent may appoint the Head of SKPD whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the protection of women and children to conduct guidance and supervision.

#### Article 27

The community may conduct supervision of the provision of protection for women and children in the regional area through the mechanism of delivering aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

# CHAPTER XII

INVESTIGATION

#### Article 28

(1) In addition to general investigation officials, investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children in this Regional Regulation may also be conducted by Civil Servant Investigators whose appointment is in accordance with statutory regulations.

(2) Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are authorized to:

- a. receive reports or complaints from a person regarding the existence of criminal acts of violence against women and children;
- b. take first action at that time at the scene and conduct examination;
- c. interrogate a suspect and examine the suspect's identification;
- d. confiscate objects and/or documents; e. take fingerprints and photograph a person;
- f. summon persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;
- g. bring in experts required in connection with case examination;
- h. terminate the investigation after receiving instruction from the General Investigator that there is insufficient evidence, or the incident does not constitute a criminal act and subsequently through the General Investigator notify the public prosecutor, suspect or their family of such matter; and/or
- i. take other necessary actions for the smooth investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children, which according to law can be accounted for.
  - (3) Investigators as referred to in paragraph (2) notify the commencement of investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the public prosecutor through the Police Official Investigator of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Law.

# CHAPTER XIII

SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

(1) Any person who intentionally fails to provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of acts of violence, allows acts of violence to occur, and/or fails to report and provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in statutory regulations.

(2) If appointed officials or P3A Officials to administer protection fail to carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with provisions of statutory regulations.

(3) P3A Officials who carry out service and protection duties toward Children and Women from acts of violence and violate service principles may be subject to sanctions in accordance with internal organizational mechanisms.

# CHAPTER XIV

SPECIAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

(1) Handling of legal cases in the investigation process by law enforcement officials must be handled by special investigators who have been designated according to their competence by law enforcement institutions for the field of protection of women and children.

(2) Special investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are:

- a. in investigation cases for women or female children who are victims of violence must be handled by female investigators;
- b. the implementation process as referred to in letter a must be in a closed room attended only by the victim's parents or guardian along with the advocacy and psychology team from P3A of the same gender;
- c. in investigation cases for male children who are victims of violence may be conducted by male or female investigators, preferably of the same gender and conducted as referred to in letter b; and
- d. Trial proceedings are not open to the public.

#### Article 31

In the case of trial proceedings related to cases of violence against children, the presence of the child does not necessarily have to be presented at the trial, except in very compelling circumstances while still separating the meeting between the child and the perpetrator in trial proceedings at different times.

#### Article 32

In the case where the perpetrator of a criminal act is a child, the following matters apply:

- a. the legal process is conducted humanely with the view that the child still has hope for their future;
- b. Investigators must be persons who have competence in special investigation for child handling cases; c. Investigation is conducted in a closed room and attended only by the advocacy team and the child's parents/guardian; d. in the investigation process, the child is not placed in general detention, but rather entrusted to their family with supervision and custody; e. the trial process is conducted specifically in the juvenile court room; and
- f. the child punishment process is placed in special child correctional institutions, with a transition period that may be accompanied by their parents.

#### Article 33

News media are not permitted to expose news coverage for cases concerning child victims of sexual violence in order to protect the child's identity and their hope for future life unless the victim as a result of such violence loses their life.

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

The role of women is very important because they are the mothers of the nation who are the pillars of the family to build the future of human resources in various fields including family economic empowerment, as well as children who are the nation's generation who will play a role in fulfilling development in the future.

Children and Women must receive protection from acts of violence, exploitation, human trafficking and neglect by responsible parties. Protection is intended to create a peaceful and prosperous atmosphere for all levels of society in fostering life and togetherness within the regional territory.

Violence cannot be avoided just like that, but must be prevented as early as possible from spreading to a level that is detrimental to all parties. Protection efforts are prioritized on prevention efforts and minimizing incidents.

Acts of violence against Children and Women constitute violations of human rights so that their self-respect and dignity need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. So far, statutory regulations governing the protection of Children and Women who are victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the Region so that institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure its implementation. The Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims specifically in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Children and Women who are victims of violence in Kotabaru Regency. As one of the efforts to protect Children and Women, it is carried out through guidance, training and assistance to victims and families of victims of violence. Economic empowerment is also carried out for the prevention of women as victims, which can be done by cultivating and developing micro and small enterprises in accordance with local potential. To cultivate micro and small enterprises, it is necessary to increase the capacity and capability of women through assistance and guidance so that women are motivated to start and develop their businesses.

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Paragraph (1)

Point a

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)

What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death

What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes

What is meant by "exploitation" includes:

- a. actions that exploit children economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- b. actions with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.
- c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or molestation.

What is meant by "trafficking in persons" is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, fraud, deception, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain the consent of a person who has control over that other person, whether conducted within a country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited.

What is meant by "household neglect" is:

- a. actions that result in the failure to fulfill a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
- b. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate;
- c. actions that neglect a person within the scope of one's household, whereas according to the law applicable to him or her or because of an agreement or contract he or she is obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;
- d. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting to work properly inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

What is meant by deviant behavior of children such as the use of glue aibon which can cause children to feel addicted or intoxicated because they are invited by their friends, and deviations towards free sex behavior or the use of illicit goods.
