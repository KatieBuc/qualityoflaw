# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Part One General Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

- 1. Region is Magetan Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Government of Magetan Regency.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Magetan.
- 4. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a child still in the womb.
- 5. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
- 6. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.

Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence. Protection is all actions of prevention, service and empowerment to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

9. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.

10. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.

11. Empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

12. Companion is a person who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery from victims of violence.

13. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by companions to women and children victims of violence during the service and empowerment process.

14. Counseling is the provision of assistance by a person who is expert/trained in such a way that the psychological understanding and ability of the victim and/or perpetrator increases in solving the problems faced;

15. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is technical service units in the Region that carry out integrated service functions for women and children victims of crime and/or violence, including as a center for information on women's empowerment and child protection.

16. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a form of UPT established by the Regional Government that functions as an information center, service center and empowerment center for women and children. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.

18. Household is family members and relatives including among others grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins and non-relatives including among others housekeepers and drivers who live and eat from 1 (one) kitchen and reside in 1 (one) house.

## Part Two

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is based on:

- a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. best interests of the victim; and
- e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:

- a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children;
- b. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- c. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;
- d. provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and e. empower women and fulfill the rights of children victims of violence.

CHAPTER II

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect;
- e. exploitation; and/or
- f. other violence.

CHAPTER III

RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5

The implementation of protection for victims of violence is a shared responsibility of: a. regional government; b. community; c. family; and d. parents.

#### Article 6

(1) The Regional Government is responsible for carrying out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence and protection of victims of violence.

(2) Prevention of the occurrence of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) in the form of:

- a. collecting data and information about women and children and related laws and regulations;
- b. conducting education on anti-violence values; and c. disseminating laws and regulations relating to the implementation of prevention and protection of victims of violence.

Protection of victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (3) (1) in the form of:

- a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving community elements;
- b. facilitating assistance, legal aid and legal services according to the needs of victims;
- c. providing housing both safe houses and alternative housing along with mechanisms for handling, services, psychosocial and spiritual;
- d. conducting continuous handling up to the stage of rehabilitation and social reintegration;
- e. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of victims of violence; and f. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of violence.

In order to carry out the responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (4) (1) the Regional Government establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in a Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence by referring to the Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence of East Java Province.

The Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women and Children

(5)

Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (2) is established for a period of 3 (three) years by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 7

The responsibility of the community as referred to in Article 5 letter b is implemented in the form of: a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; and

b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties.

#### Article 8

Family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letters c and d have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

- a. conducting guidance towards harmonious household integrity;

# CHAPTER VI

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 21

(1)In the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence, every victim receives the following rights: a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected;

b. the right to recovery of health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by victims;

c. the right to determine their own decisions;

d. the right to obtain information;

e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;

f. the right to social rehabilitation;

g. the right to complaint handling;

h. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or

i. the right to accompaniment.

(2) Children who are victims of violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in paragraph (1) also receive special rights, as follows: a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;

b. the right to basic services;

c. the right to obtain spiritual guidance;

d. the right to equal protection;

e. the right to be free from stigma; and/or

f. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER VII

Article 22

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of: a.forming family partners at the village/sub-district level; b.forming women's and children's protection units within community organizations;

c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;

d. providing first aid to victims; and e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

CHAPTER VIII

#### Article 23

(2) Reporting on the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence is conducted 1 (one) time per year and is coordinated by regional apparatus organizations that administer affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore women and children need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life must be guaranteed in accordance with their nature and calling without discrimination.

Violence against women and children not only causes physical impacts but also psychological impacts which in general can affect the quality of life of women and children victims of violence.

On a national scale, the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1948 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women). In addition, the Government has also enacted Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606) and Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419).

Furthermore, based on Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 and Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as has been amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015, women's empowerment and child protection constitute governmental affairs which must be implemented by Regional Governments.

As has occurred in various regions, including Magetan Regency, there is currently a phenomenon of increasing occurrence of acts of violence against women and children. In order to address this matter, regional regulations are needed which provide a basis for the Regional Government and/or the community in realizing protection, so that victims are prevented and freed from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Magetan Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, protection must be provided for women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation. Therefore, policies are needed that can guarantee protection to free them from acts of violence, as well as improve their quality of life. Based on the above considerations, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Magetan Regency concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

#### Article 4

Point a

Physical violence referred to is caused by acts that result in pain, illness, or serious injury and or result in injury, or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Forms of physical violence include slapping, hitting, spitting, pulling hair/yanking hair, kicking, burning with hot objects, hitting/injuring with weapons.

Point b

Psychological violence referred to is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Forms of psychological violence may include emotional abuse such as insults, hurtful comments or degrading self-esteem, isolating the wife from the outside world, threatening or, intimidating as a means of imposing one's will

Point c

Sexual violence referred to is caused by:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
- b. coercion of sexual relations;
- c. coercion of sexual relations in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
- d. coercion of sexual relations with other people for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Point d

Neglect referred to is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as they should be committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
- c. acts of abandoning a person within their household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Point e

Exploitation referred to is caused by:

- a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- b. acts with or without the consent of the victim including prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to gain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
- c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to gain profit, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

Point f

Other violence referred to is caused by:

- a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and/or
- b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
