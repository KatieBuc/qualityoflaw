5.1 | Information and education about VAW | Does the national legal framework include information, awareness, communication, or education activities specifically about violence against women as a primary prevention measure?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- information, education, awareness-raising, public communication, campaigns, socialisation, advocacy, or similar activities are explicitly directed at violence against women, gender-based violence, the prevention of violence, protection from violence, the harms or consequences of violence, or norms rejecting violence against women;
- the framework requires public awareness, community education, dissemination of information, or communication activities specifically concerning violence against women or preventing violence against women;
- such information or education activities are expressly identified as prevention measures, prevention programmes, prevention strategies, or prevention responsibilities.

Code Yes - Referenced only if information, awareness, communication, or education about violence against women is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument, without an operative prevention provision.

Code No if the framework only refers to:

- general education, information, or awareness activities unrelated to violence against women;
- women's rights, victims' rights, or gender equality without linking the activity to violence prevention;
- laws, regulations, or policies concerning violence against women without an information or education activity;
- education as a general right or service; or
- general socialisation, communication, or education that is not linked to preventing violence against women or protecting women from violence.

Do not infer that general awareness or education prevents violence. The framework must explicitly link the activity to violence against women, prevention of violence, or protection from violence.



5.2 | Information and education about gender equality | Does the national legal framework include primary prevention activities that provide information, education, awareness raising, communication, or similar educational activities specifically about gender equality or women's rights?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework explicitly requires or provides prevention activities that educate or raise awareness about gender equality, gender justice, equal rights of women and men, women's rights, or protection of women's rights;
- the framework provides education or awareness activities addressing non-discrimination against women, elimination of discrimination, or gender-sensitive or gender-responsive attitudes, norms, or behaviours;
- such activities include public awareness campaigns, community or school education, information and communication activities, advocacy, socialisation, training, or similar educational measures;
- the framework explicitly identifies these activities as a strategy or measure for preventing violence against women.

Code Yes - Referenced only if information, education, or awareness about gender equality or women's rights as a means of preventing violence against women is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework only:

- recognises gender equality or women's rights as a principle, objective, or policy aim without requiring an educational or awareness activity;
- promotes women's empowerment without an information, education, awareness, or communication component;
- refers to gender-responsive services, gender mainstreaming, or gender-responsive budgeting without an educational or awareness activity;
- provides information or education about violence against women rather than gender equality or women's rights;
- provides information or education about VAW laws and policies; or
- mentions education or awareness activities for children only in relation to preventing violence against children.

Do not infer that a general commitment to gender equality or women's rights constitutes an educational or awareness-raising activity. The framework must provide an information, education, awareness, communication, or similar activity and explicitly link it to the prevention of violence against women.



5.3 | Information and education about VAW laws and policies | Does the national legal framework include primary prevention activities providing information, education, or awareness about laws and policies concerning violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework explicitly provides for dissemination, socialisation, education, awareness-raising, public information, or communication activities concerning laws, regulations, policies, or legal protections related to violence against women;
- the framework provides public education or legal-awareness activities concerning legal rights, protections, remedies, or obligations relating to violence against women;
- such activities target communities, the public, institutions, or other relevant groups and are explicitly concerned with VAW laws, regulations, policies, or legal protections;
- the framework expressly identifies awareness or dissemination of VAW laws or policies as a prevention strategy or measure.

Code Yes - Referenced only if information, education, awareness, or dissemination of VAW laws or policies is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument, without an operative prevention provision.

Code No if:

- the framework only provides services or responses after violence occurs without an information or education component;
- awareness or education activities are mentioned but are unrelated to VAW laws, regulations, policies, or legal protections;
- the framework merely establishes or describes VAW laws without providing for their dissemination or public education; or
- general legal-awareness activities are mentioned without specifying that they concern laws, regulations, policies, or legal protections relating to violence against women.

Do not infer that a general legal-awareness, information, or communication provision covers VAW laws. The framework must specify that the information or education concerns laws, regulations, policies, or legal protections related to violence against women.



5.4 | Prevention through education and improving the quality of education | Does the national legal framework explicitly identify education, educational institutions, curricula, or improvements in the quality of education as a primary prevention measure for violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework explicitly identifies educational programmes, school-based initiatives, curricula, teaching and learning materials, or educational institutions as measures to prevent violence against women;
- the framework requires education institutions, schools, universities, learning centres, or education authorities to implement measures designed to prevent violence against women;
- the framework provides for the development or improvement of education, educational content, curricula, teaching materials, or educational standards where these measures are explicitly linked to preventing violence against women;
- the framework includes education-based prevention programmes addressing women's rights, gender equality, violence against women, non-violence, or related norms, where the educational measure is expressly presented as preventing violence;
- the framework assigns responsibility to education authorities or institutions to implement education-based measures for preventing violence against women.

Code Yes - Referenced only if education, educational institutions, curricula, or improvement of education as a means of preventing violence against women is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework only:

- expands access to education or establishes compulsory education without linking it to prevention of violence;
- establishes schools, universities, scholarships, or educational institutions without a violence-prevention function;
- provides formal or non-formal education without explicitly linking it to prevention of violence against women;
- provides vocational, skills, employment, entrepreneurship, or political education without linking it to prevention of violence against women;
- promotes women's empowerment through education without identifying education as a violence-prevention measure;
- generally improves the quality, availability, or accessibility of education without explicitly linking these measures to preventing violence against women;
- mentions broad education, training, capacity building, socialisation, or awareness activities without identifying a formal education mechanism or institution; or
- identifies education as a measure to prevent violence against children only, without extending the measure to violence against women.

Do not infer that education prevents violence. Code Yes - Substantive only where the framework explicitly identifies education, educational institutions, educational content, or improvement of education as a measure or strategy to prevent violence against women.



5.5 | Engagement of men and boys | Does the national legal framework include primary prevention activities that engage men and boys to prevent violence against women, including through positive masculinity or gender-norm change programmes?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework explicitly requires or provides programmes, campaigns, education, training, or other prevention activities targeting men or boys to prevent violence against women;
- the framework includes measures addressing harmful gender norms, masculinities, attitudes, behaviours, or social expectations among men or boys as a means of preventing violence against women;
- the framework promotes positive, non-violent, gender-equitable, or respectful masculinities among men or boys as a violence-prevention measure;
- the framework engages men or boys as participants, advocates, educators, community leaders, or agents of change in activities explicitly aimed at preventing violence against women;
- the framework establishes community, school, workplace, or other programmes involving men or boys that are expressly intended to change attitudes, norms, or behaviours associated with violence against women.

Code Yes - Referenced only if engagement of men or boys in preventing violence against women, including changing gender norms or masculinities, is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework only:

- identifies men and boys as potential perpetrators without establishing prevention or engagement activities;
- provides general education, awareness, or gender-equality programmes without specifically engaging men or boys;
- promotes gender equality generally without a measure targeting men or boys;
- provides programmes for male perpetrators after violence has occurred, without a primary prevention component aimed at preventing violence before it occurs;
- refers to men's participation in family, community, or social activities without linking that participation to preventing violence against women; or
- mentions boys only in relation to preventing violence against children without addressing violence against women.

Do not require the framework to use the terms "positive masculinity," "masculinities," or "gender norms." An operative measure specifically engaging men or boys to change attitudes, behaviours, norms, or practices for the prevention of violence against women is sufficient.
