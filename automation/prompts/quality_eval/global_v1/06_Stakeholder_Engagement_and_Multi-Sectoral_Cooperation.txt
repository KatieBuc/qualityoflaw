6.1 | Engagement/collaboration with civil society organisations and NGOs | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with civil society organisations or non-governmental organisations in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, requires, or authorises government agencies or other responsible institutions to cooperate or collaborate with civil society organisations or NGOs on violence against women;
- civil society organisations or NGOs are identified as partners in the prevention of, protection from, or response to violence against women;
- the framework provides for the participation of civil society organisations or NGOs in service delivery, referral mechanisms, policy implementation, awareness-raising, monitoring, research, or other VAW-related activities;
- the framework establishes consultation, partnership, coordination, contracting, funding, accreditation, or other formal mechanisms through which civil society organisations or NGOs participate in implementing VAW measures.

Code Yes - Referenced only if collaboration or engagement with civil society organisations or NGOs is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument, without an operative provision.

Code No if the framework:
- merely recognises the importance of civil society or NGOs without establishing or requiring engagement;
- refers to NGOs or civil society generally without linking them to prevention of or response to violence against women; or
- does not mention engagement or collaboration with civil society organisations or NGOs in relation to VAW.

Do not require a dedicated VAW NGO partnership. Any operative provision establishing or authorising collaboration with civil society organisations or NGOs for VAW-related prevention, protection, response, or implementation is sufficient.



6.2 | Engagement/collaboration with religious and traditional organisations or leaders | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with religious organisations or leaders, or traditional/cultural organisations or leaders, in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, requires, or authorises cooperation or collaboration with religious organisations, religious leaders, traditional leaders, cultural organisations, or community/customary leaders on violence against women;
- religious or traditional/cultural organisations or leaders are identified as partners in preventing violence against women, supporting victims, changing harmful norms or practices, or facilitating access to services;
- the framework provides for their participation in awareness-raising, education, community mobilisation, referral, mediation or support services, prevention programmes, or implementation of VAW policies;
- the framework establishes a formal consultation, coordination, partnership, or participation mechanism involving religious or traditional/cultural actors in relation to VAW.

Code Yes - Referenced only if engagement with religious or traditional/cultural organisations or leaders is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework:
- merely refers to religion, culture, tradition, or customary practices without establishing engagement with relevant organisations or leaders;
- identifies religious or traditional leaders generally without linking them to VAW prevention or response; or
- does not mention collaboration or engagement with these actors in relation to VAW.

Do not require both religious and traditional/cultural actors to be covered. Engagement with either category is sufficient for Yes - Substantive.



6.3 | Engagement/collaboration with education institutions | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with education institutions, including universities, in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- education institutions are identified as partners in VAW prevention, education, awareness-raising, research, service provision, referral, or implementation of VAW policies;
- the framework assigns education institutions responsibilities to participate in programmes, campaigns, training, research, data collection, referral, or other VAW-related activities;
- the framework establishes a formal mechanism for coordination or partnership between government authorities and education institutions concerning VAW.

Code Yes - Referenced only if engagement or collaboration with education institutions in relation to VAW is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework:
- merely establishes or regulates education institutions without linking them to VAW prevention or response;
- refers generally to education or educational programmes without identifying education institutions as participants or partners; or
- does not mention engagement or collaboration with education institutions in relation to VAW.

Do not require a university or other specific type of institution. Engagement with any formal education institution is sufficient.



6.4 | Engagement/collaboration with international organisations | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with international organisations in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, requires, or authorises cooperation with international organisations on violence against women;
- international organisations are identified as partners in implementing, financing, monitoring, researching, developing, or evaluating VAW policies or programmes;
- the framework provides for technical assistance, institutional cooperation, joint programmes, information exchange, capacity building, or other forms of collaboration with international organisations specifically concerning VAW;
- the framework establishes a mechanism through which national authorities cooperate with international or regional organisations on VAW prevention, protection, response, or implementation.

Code Yes - Referenced only if cooperation with international organisations concerning VAW is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework:
- merely refers to international standards, treaties, conventions, or international law without establishing collaboration with an international organisation;
- mentions membership in or ratification of an international instrument without an operative cooperation mechanism; or
- does not mention engagement or collaboration with international organisations in relation to VAW.

Do not treat ratification of an international or regional instrument, by itself, as collaboration with an international organisation.

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework establishes responsibilities, partnerships, guidelines, or mechanisms for media engagement specifically concerning prevention of violence against women or protection of victims;

- regulates media generally without linking the regulation or engagement to VAW prevention or response; or

Do not require a formal media partnership. An operative provision establishing or authorising media engagement for VAW-related prevention, awareness, or response is sufficient.



6.6 | Engagement/collaboration with communities and society | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with communities, community organisations, or members of the public in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, requires, or authorises community participation or engagement in preventing or responding to violence against women;
- communities, community organisations, community groups, or members of the public are identified as partners in VAW prevention, awareness-raising, victim support, referral, protection, or monitoring;
- the framework provides for community mobilisation, community-based prevention programmes, public participation, or community referral or support mechanisms specifically addressing VAW;
- the framework assigns responsibilities or establishes mechanisms for community participation in implementing VAW policies or programmes.

Code Yes - Referenced only if community or public engagement in relation to VAW is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework:
- merely refers to society, communities, or the public in general terms without establishing an engagement mechanism;
- provides general public services or awareness activities without identifying community participation or collaboration; or
- does not mention community or public engagement in relation to VAW.

Do not infer community engagement merely from provisions requiring the public to comply with the law or report violence. The framework must establish or recognise community participation, collaboration, mobilisation, or another form of active engagement in VAW prevention or response.



6.7 | Engagement/collaboration with business and private sector | Does the national legal framework establish or require engagement or collaboration with businesses or private-sector actors in the prevention of or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, requires, or authorises cooperation with businesses, employers, private companies, industry organisations, or other private-sector actors on violence against women;
- private-sector actors are identified as partners in VAW prevention, awareness-raising, victim support, economic recovery, employment, referral, or service provision;
- the framework requires or encourages employers or businesses to participate in measures addressing violence against women, including workplace violence, prevention programmes, victim support, or referral mechanisms;
- the framework establishes partnerships, agreements, funding arrangements, corporate participation, or other formal mechanisms involving the private sector in VAW prevention or response.

Code Yes - Referenced only if private-sector engagement in relation to VAW is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or as part of the cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument.

Code No if the framework:
- merely regulates employers or businesses without linking their obligations or participation to VAW;
- promotes women's employment or economic empowerment generally without involving the private sector in VAW prevention or response;
- refers generally to public-private cooperation without specifying a VAW-related purpose; or
- does not mention engagement or collaboration with business or private-sector actors in relation to VAW.

Do not require the private sector to provide dedicated VAW services. An operative provision establishing or authorising private-sector participation in VAW prevention, protection, response, victim support, or implementation is sufficient.
