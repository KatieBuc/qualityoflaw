3.1 | Institutional and sectoral coordination across agencies and jurisdictions | Does the national legal framework establish an institutional coordination mechanism across agencies, sectors, or jurisdictions to support the implementation of prevention, protection, or response to violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if the framework establishes, in its operative provisions, any formal coordination or cooperation mechanism, including:
- coordination or cooperation between government agencies, ministries, or departments;
- cross-sectoral or multi-agency coordination;
- institutional networks, integrated service networks, referral networks, task forces, committees, or coordination forums;
- coordination between different levels or tiers of government (e.g. national, regional/state, local);
- coordination or cooperation between different jurisdictions;
- a national action plan or strategy on violence against women with a designated coordinating body or implementation structure.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework states that agencies or sectors shall coordinate or work together, without establishing a formal mechanism, body, or process to carry this out, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if the framework merely assigns responsibilities to individual agencies without establishing coordination, cooperation, or a network between them, or does not mention coordination at all.

Do not require cross-jurisdictional coordination for Substantive - coordination within a single level of government or between sectors is sufficient.


3.2 | Data collection or information system | Does the national legal framework establish, require, or assign responsibility for a system or entity to collect, document, record, monitor, report, manage, or exchange data or information on violence against women, victims, cases, or women's protection?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework establishes or mandates a violence against women or women's protection data or information system;
- the framework requires the collection, recording, documentation, monitoring, reporting, management, or exchange of data or information on violence, victims, or cases;
- the framework assigns an agency or entity responsibility for maintaining, developing, or managing data or information on violence against women, victims, or cases.

A dedicated institution, unit, or data management system is not necessary for Substantive - it is sufficient that the framework explicitly assigns a function, duty, or responsibility for collecting, recording, reporting, monitoring, exchanging, or managing data on violence, violence cases, or victims.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework expresses an intent to develop, strengthen, or improve data or information systems on violence against women generally, without assigning a specific duty, function, or responsible entity, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if the framework only:
- gives victims a right to receive information;
- establishes referral or communication networks without a data or information function; or
- requires ordinary administrative reporting that does not involve collecting or managing violence against women case data.


3.3 | Gender mainstreaming institutionalisation | Does the national legal framework establish or require gender mainstreaming as an institutional mechanism for implementing women's protection or violence against women policies and programs?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- gender mainstreaming is explicitly required or implemented as part of prevention, protection, planning, implementation, or service delivery;
- the framework establishes a gender mainstreaming working group, focal point, institution, or coordination mechanism;
- gender mainstreaming is integrated into government planning, budgeting, or institutional responsibilities relevant to violence against women; or
- institutional strengthening or coordination is explicitly directed at implementing gender mainstreaming.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework expresses a general commitment to gender equality, gender justice, or gender-responsive approaches without establishing or requiring a specific gender mainstreaming mechanism, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if the framework refers only to:
- gender equality, gender justice, or gender-responsive budgeting without mentioning gender mainstreaming; or
- women's empowerment or gender-related objectives without establishing or requiring gender mainstreaming as an implementation mechanism.


3.4 | Training and capacity building | Does the national legal framework require or provide for training, technical guidance, or capacity building for government officials, implementing agencies, or service providers responsible for implementing violence against women policy?

Code Yes - Substantive if the framework explicitly mandates or provides for training, technical guidance, professional development, or capacity building for government officials, public servants, police, health workers, social workers, legal personnel, counsellors, or other personnel responsible for implementing, coordinating, or delivering services under the framework.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework references training or capacity building as a general governmental authority or aspiration, without a corresponding duty or mandate directed at implementing personnel, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if:
- the framework refers only to services being delivered by "trained officers" or "trained personnel," or describes staff qualifications, without mandating training or capacity building;
- training or empowerment activities (job training, entrepreneurship training, livelihood training, awareness raising) are directed at victims or the community rather than implementing personnel;
- the framework refers generally to "capacity building" without indicating that it applies to government institutions, implementing agencies, or service providers;
- the framework merely references guidance, guidelines, instructions, or supervision without a training or capacity-building component; or
- training or capacity building is mandated only in relation to child protection and is not explicitly extended to violence against women or gender-based violence.


3.5 | Monitoring and evaluation | Does the national legal framework establish monitoring and evaluation of policy implementation and service delivery for violence against women?

Code Yes - Substantive if the operative provisions of any relevant instrument establish a specific monitoring and evaluation mechanism, process, indicator framework, or reporting requirement, or designate a responsible body for assessing the implementation of violence against women law, policy, or service delivery.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework states generally that implementation "shall be monitored and evaluated," without specifying a mechanism, process, or responsible entity, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if monitoring and evaluation of violence against women policy implementation or service delivery is not mentioned anywhere in the framework.


3.6 | National budget allocation | Does the national legal framework require the allocation of a budget to fund the implementation of violence against women response and prevention?

Code Yes - Substantive if an operative provision of any relevant instrument creates a binding requirement to allocate budget or funding - from the national budget, a dedicated fund, or another specified source - for implementing violence against women response services or prevention initiatives.

Code Yes - Referenced only if the framework states generally that government "shall provide necessary resources" or "adequate funding shall be made available," without a binding requirement, specified source, or designated mechanism, or if it appears solely in a preamble or explanatory section.

Code No if no funding or budget-allocation provision for implementing violence against women response or prevention is mentioned anywhere in the framework.
