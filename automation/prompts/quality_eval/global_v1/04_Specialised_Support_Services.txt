4.1 | Integrated service unit for VAW | Does the national legal framework establish, mandate, or recognise an integrated service mechanism for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework explicitly establishes, mandates, or recognises an integrated service unit, one-stop centre, integrated service centre, multidisciplinary service mechanism, or equivalent mechanism specifically for women victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework establishes a dedicated mechanism that coordinates or provides multiple services for VAW victims, such as health/medical care, legal assistance, psychosocial support, shelter, protection, police assistance, referral, or case management;
- the framework assigns responsibility to a government agency, institution, service provider, or designated mechanism to provide or coordinate integrated services for women victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework requires cooperation or integration among multiple victim-support services through a designated institutional mechanism.

Code Yes - Referenced only if an integrated or coordinated service approach for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratifying a relevant international/regional instrument, without an operative provision establishing, mandating, or recognising such a mechanism.

Code No if:
- the framework only provides for individual services without an integrated or coordinated victim-support mechanism;
- it refers generally to coordination, cooperation, or victim support without establishing or recognising an integrated service mechanism; or
- no integrated service mechanism for women victims/survivors of violence is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

Do not require a standalone physical centre. An operative provision establishing an integrated or multidisciplinary service mechanism is sufficient.


4.2 | Reporting/hotline service | Does the national legal framework provide for a mechanism or service through which women victims/survivors of violence can report violence, seek assistance, or access emergency support?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework establishes or mandates a hotline, helpline, telephone service, emergency line, online reporting channel, complaint mechanism, or equivalent service for victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework establishes a designated service, authority, institution, or mechanism responsible for receiving complaints or reports of violence from victims or the public;
- the framework provides a specific reporting, referral, or assistance mechanism through which women experiencing violence can seek protection or support;
- an integrated victim-support mechanism is expressly authorised or required to receive reports or complaints and provide or arrange assistance.

Code Yes - Referenced only if reporting, hotline, complaint, or help-seeking mechanisms for victims of violence are mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification, without an operative provision.

Code No if:
- the framework only requires administrative reporting or inter-agency reporting of VAW cases;
- it merely states that victims may report violence to authorities without establishing or recognising a reporting mechanism or service;
- it refers to general emergency or public-service hotlines without linking them to violence against women or victim assistance; or
- no mechanism for victims to report violence or seek assistance is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

A dedicated VAW hotline is not required if the framework establishes another operative reporting or help-seeking mechanism specifically applicable to victims of violence.



4.3 | Safety/protection shelter | Does the national legal framework provide for safe accommodation, shelter, or other residential protection for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework establishes, mandates, or recognises shelters, safe houses, refuge centres, crisis accommodation, temporary accommodation, protection residences, or equivalent facilities for women victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework requires an authority, institution, or service provider to provide emergency or temporary accommodation for victims of violence;
- shelter or safe accommodation is included as part of an integrated or specialised victim-support service;
- the framework provides for relocation, residential protection, or a safe place for victims at risk of further violence.

Code Yes - Referenced only if shelter, safe accommodation, refuge, or residential protection for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification.

Code No if:
- the framework refers only to general protection, housing, welfare, or social assistance without providing accommodation-based protection for VAW victims;
- it establishes victim services but does not include shelter or safe accommodation; or
- no shelter, safe house, refuge, or equivalent residential protection mechanism is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

A standalone shelter is not required. Shelter provided through an integrated service mechanism or another designated institution is sufficient.



4.4 | Legal assistance | Does the national legal framework provide legal assistance or legal aid to women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework establishes or guarantees access to legal aid, legal assistance, legal advice, legal representation, or legal counselling for women victims/survivors of violence;
- victims of violence are expressly identified as eligible for publicly funded or otherwise supported legal assistance;
- the framework requires an authority, legal aid institution, lawyer, or designated service provider to provide legal assistance to victims of violence;
- legal assistance is included within an integrated or specialised victim-support service.

Code Yes - Referenced only if access to legal assistance for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification, without an operative entitlement, duty, or service mechanism.

Code No if:
- the framework merely guarantees general access to justice without providing or identifying legal assistance for victims of violence;
- it provides general legal aid to disadvantaged persons without explicitly linking it to women victims/survivors of violence; or
- no legal assistance mechanism or entitlement for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

Do not require free legal assistance. An operative provision providing legal assistance, legal advice, or representation is sufficient.



4.5 | Health/medical services | Does the national legal framework provide health or medical services specifically for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework requires or provides access to medical examination, treatment, emergency care, reproductive health services, forensic medical services, or other health services for victims/survivors of violence;
- health facilities or health professionals are assigned responsibility for treating, examining, referring, or supporting women victims/survivors of violence;
- medical or health services are expressly included within victim-support, protection, recovery, or referral mechanisms;
- the framework establishes protocols or procedures requiring health services to respond to VAW cases.

Code Yes - Referenced only if health or medical assistance for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification.

Code No if:
- the framework only recognises a general right to health;
- it provides general health services without linking them to victims/survivors of violence;
- violence-related health consequences are mentioned without establishing or requiring a health/medical response; or
- no health or medical service for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

General health-system obligations are not sufficient unless the framework expressly connects them to victims/survivors of violence.



4.6 | Psychosocial counselling services | Does the national legal framework provide psychosocial, psychological, counselling, or trauma-support services for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- psychosocial counselling, psychological counselling, psychological support, therapy, trauma counselling, mental-health support, or equivalent services are provided to victims/survivors of violence;
- psychologists, counsellors, social workers, therapists, or other relevant professionals are assigned responsibility for providing psychosocial or psychological support to victims;
- psychosocial or psychological services are included within victim assistance, rehabilitation, recovery, protection, or integrated service mechanisms;
- the framework establishes referral or support mechanisms specifically for the psychosocial or psychological needs of VAW victims.

Code Yes - Referenced only if psychosocial, psychological, counselling, or trauma support for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification.

Code No if:
- the framework refers only to general rehabilitation or recovery without a psychosocial or psychological component;
- psychological harm, trauma, or mental-health consequences are recognised without providing a corresponding service; or
- no psychosocial, psychological, counselling, or equivalent support service for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the framework.



4.7 | Religious/spiritual counselling | Does the national legal framework provide religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, or faith-based support for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, mentoring, pastoral care, or equivalent faith-based support is expressly provided to victims/survivors of violence;
- religious leaders, faith-based counsellors, chaplains, clerics, or equivalent personnel are identified as providing counselling or support to victims;
- religious or spiritual support forms part of victim assistance, counselling, rehabilitation, recovery, or integrated services;
- a religious or faith-based institution is assigned responsibility for providing counselling, guidance, or support to women victims/survivors of violence.

Code Yes - Referenced only if religious or spiritual support for victims of violence is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification.

Code No if:
- religion or spirituality is mentioned only as a general principle or cultural consideration;
- religious organisations are mentioned without a counselling or victim-support function;
- the framework refers to "spiritual" or "mental and spiritual" matters without establishing a support service; or
- no religious or spiritual counselling or support service for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

A dedicated religious/spiritual service unit is not required. An operative provision providing such support through an existing institution or service is sufficient.



4.8 | Recovery and support for victims | Does the national legal framework provide recovery, rehabilitation, reintegration, or other longer-term support for women victims/survivors of violence?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:

- the framework establishes or provides rehabilitation, recovery, reintegration, resocialisation, or other longer-term support services for victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework provides skills training, vocational training, education, employment assistance, livelihood support, financial assistance, economic support, housing support, or other measures specifically intended to support the recovery or reintegration of VAW victims;
- the framework establishes mechanisms to promote the economic independence, social reintegration, rehabilitation, or long-term recovery of women victims/survivors of violence;
- an integrated or specialised victim-support mechanism is assigned responsibility for recovery, rehabilitation, reintegration, or livelihood support.

Code Yes - Referenced only if recovery, rehabilitation, reintegration, or longer-term support is mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification, without an operative provision.

Code No if:

- rehabilitation or recovery is mentioned only as a general objective without a corresponding support measure or mechanism;
- economic empowerment, employment, skills training, or welfare programs are provided generally to women or vulnerable groups without explicitly linking them to victims/survivors of violence;
- the framework only provides immediate protection or emergency assistance, with no longer-term recovery or support measure; or
- no recovery, rehabilitation, reintegration, or related support for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the framework.
Do not require a standalone rehabilitation service. A measure incorporated into an integrated victim-support framework is sufficient.



4.9 | Perpetrator programmes | Does the national legal framework establish or provide programmes for perpetrators of violence against women, including intervention, rehabilitation, treatment, or behaviour-change programmes?

Code Yes - Substantive if, in the operative provisions of any relevant instrument:
- the framework establishes, mandates, or provides programmes for perpetrators of violence against women;
- perpetrators are required or enabled to participate in rehabilitation, intervention, counselling, treatment, behaviour-change, or similar programmes;
- the framework establishes perpetrator-focused services intended to prevent reoffending, address violent behaviour, or promote behavioural change;
- courts, correctional authorities, probation services, social services, or other designated institutions are authorised or required to refer perpetrators to or administer such programmes;
- participation in a perpetrator programme is imposed as part of a criminal sentence, protection-order process, diversion mechanism, probation measure, or other legal response to VAW.

Code Yes - Referenced only if perpetrator rehabilitation, intervention, treatment, or behaviour-change programmes are mentioned solely in a preamble, recital, explanatory section, or cited basis for ratification, without an operative provision.

Code No if:
- the framework only establishes criminal penalties, imprisonment, or other sanctions for perpetrators without providing an intervention, rehabilitation, treatment, or behaviour-change programme;
- it provides general rehabilitation or correctional services without linking them to perpetrators of violence against women;
- it focuses solely on victim rehabilitation or recovery; or
- no perpetrator intervention, rehabilitation, treatment, or behaviour-change programme is mentioned anywhere in the framework.

Do not require the programme to be mandatory or court-ordered. An operative provision establishing or providing a perpetrator-focused intervention or behaviour-change programme is sufficient.
