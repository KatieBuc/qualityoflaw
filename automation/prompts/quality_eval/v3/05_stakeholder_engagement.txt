5.2 | Religious engagement | Does the policy include religious organisations, religious leaders, clergy, spiritual counsellors, or other faith-based actors in the delivery of services, prevention activities, coordination, or implementation?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
religious organisations or faith-based organisations are identified as stakeholders or partners;
religious leaders, clergy, spiritual counsellors, religious mentors, or similar actors provide services, counselling, guidance, prevention activities, referrals, or support for survivors;
religious or spiritual counselling/guidance is included as a support service for survivors;
religious organisations are included within the definition of community organisations, community institutions, or other stakeholder groups participating in implementation or prevention;
religious organisations or leaders participate in coordination, implementation, referral mechanisms, or community prevention activities.
Code No if the policy refers only to religious values, religious norms, religious obligations, or religious principles without involving religious organisations, leaders, or faith-based service providers.
5.3 | Traditional/cultural engagement | Does the policy include traditional, customary or cultural leaders, organisations, institutions, or customary/community mechanisms in the implementation of prevention, protection, services, coordination, or dispute resolution?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
traditional, customary or cultural leaders are identified as stakeholders, participants or partners;
community leaders or community figures are explicitly identified in contexts where they function as customary or traditional leaders;
customary, traditional or cultural institutions participate in prevention, services, referrals, implementation or coordination;
the policy recognises customary or traditional mechanisms for resolving violence or protecting victims;
customary or traditional institutions are included within broader community organisations or implementation structures.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
culture, customs or traditions as general principles;
preserving local culture or traditions;
local wisdom without linking it to implementation, prevention, services, coordination or customary institutions;
general community participation where traditional/customary leaders or institutions are not identified or reasonably implied.

5.6 | Media engagement | Does the policy engage or recognise the media as a stakeholder in prevention, protection, or services?
Code Yes if the policy:
engages or collaborates with the media or mass media;
assigns the media a role in prevention, protection, service delivery, awareness, or capacity building;
includes the media within the definition of community, community organisations, or stakeholder networks.
Code No if the policy only mentions the media without assigning it a role in implementing prevention, protection, or services.

5.8 | Private sector engagement | Does the policy engage or recognise the business/private sector as a stakeholder in prevention, protection, or services?
Code Yes if the policy:
engages or collaborates with businesses, employers, business associations, or the private sector;
identifies business/private organisations as part of the community or stakeholder network;
assigns a role to the private sector in prevention, protection, or service delivery.
Code No if the policy only refers to businesses in relation to employment, workplaces, or economic activities without assigning a stakeholder role.
