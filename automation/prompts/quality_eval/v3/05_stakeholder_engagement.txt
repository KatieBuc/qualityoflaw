5.2 | Religious engagement | Does the policy include religious organisations, religious leaders, clergy, spiritual counsellors, or other faith-based actors in the delivery of services, prevention activities, coordination, or implementation?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
religious organisations or faith-based organisations are identified as stakeholders or partners;
religious leaders, clergy, spiritual counsellors, religious mentors, or similar actors provide services, counselling, guidance, prevention activities, referrals, or support for survivors;
religious or spiritual counselling/guidance is included as a support service for survivors;
religious organisations are included within the definition of community organisations, community institutions, or other stakeholder groups participating in implementation or prevention;
religious organisations or leaders participate in coordination, implementation, referral mechanisms, or community prevention activities.
Code No if the policy refers only to religious values, religious norms, religious obligations, or religious principles without involving religious organisations, leaders, or faith-based service providers.
