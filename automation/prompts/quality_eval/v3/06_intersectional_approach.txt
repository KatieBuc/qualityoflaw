6.2 | Women victims of trafficking | Does the policy include women victims of human trafficking (including trafficking or exploitation) within its scope?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
women or victims of trafficking/exploitation are included in the definitions of victims or protected persons;
trafficking victims are explicitly mentioned anywhere in the policy;
trafficking victims are included in services, protection measures, institutional responsibilities, coordination mechanisms, implementation provisions, or task force responsibilities;
the policy cites national human trafficking legislation (or equivalent trafficking laws) in the legal foundation, "Considering", "Observing", "Bearing in Mind", or similar introductory legal references. Such references indicate that trafficking cases and victims are intended to be covered by the policy, even if trafficking-specific protection measures are not subsequently described.
Code No only if trafficking or trafficking victims are not mentioned anywhere in the policy.
Do not require trafficking-specific services or dedicated protection measures. Any explicit inclusion of trafficking victims, including through definitions, legal foundations, or implementation provisions, is sufficient for a Yes.
6.3 | Women workers | Does the policy explicitly provide protection or special measures for women workers or employees as a specific target group?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly identifies women workers, female employees, working women, or women in employment as a group receiving specific protection or services.
Code No if the policy only:
recognises women's right to work or equal employment;
provides general labour or employment rights (e.g. maternity leave, lactation, wages, workplace benefits);
promotes women's employment, empowerment, education, or workplace participation;
refers generally to women without specifically protecting women workers as a target group.

6.8 | Women in legal process | Does the policy explicitly provide protection or services for women in conflict with the law as suspects, defendants, perpetrators, or witnesses?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly provides protections or services for women in conflict with the law as witnesses or alleged/perpetrating offenders.
Do not code Yes if the policy only provides:
legal aid or court assistance for women victims;
victim or witness protection for victims of violence;
investigation or judicial procedures for victims;
general legal process rights applying to victims.
Do not infer that protections for women victims in legal proceedings apply to women in conflict with the law.
