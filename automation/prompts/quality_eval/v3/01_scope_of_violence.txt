1.8 | Human trafficking | Does the policy include human trafficking or trafficking in persons?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
human trafficking, trafficking in persons, trafficking, or exploitation is explicitly mentioned;
victims of trafficking or trafficking for exploitation are included in definitions or target groups;
human trafficking is included as a form of violence, abuse, exploitation, or offence addressed by the policy;
the policy cites human trafficking legislation or regulations in its legal basis (considering/observing/legal foundation);
services, protection, prevention, recovery, or institutional responsibilities include trafficking victims.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
exploitation without any indication that it refers to trafficking or trafficking in persons;
general violence or victim protection with no mention of trafficking or trafficking victims.
