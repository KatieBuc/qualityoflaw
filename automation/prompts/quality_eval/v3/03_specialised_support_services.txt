3.8 | Religious/spiritual counselling | Does the policy include religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, mentoring, or similar faith-based psychosocial support as a service available to victims?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
religious or spiritual counselling is explicitly listed as a service;
spiritual guidance, religious guidance, spiritual mentoring, religious mentoring, pastoral care, or similar support is provided to victims;
services are delivered by religious leaders, spiritual counsellors, clerics, chaplains, religious mentors, or similar personnel;
victim services are implemented by institutions or agencies responsible for mental and spiritual affairs;
religious or spiritual support forms part of rehabilitation, recovery, counselling, or integrated victim services.
A dedicated or specialised service unit is not required. Any explicit provision of religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, or support is sufficient.
Code No if the policy only:
mentions religion or spirituality as a general principle;
refers to religious organisations without describing counselling or support services;
refers to "mental and spiritual" matters without indicating the provision of support or services.
For this indicator, "spiritual guidance", "religious guidance", "spiritual services", "mental and spiritual services", and services provided by religious or spiritual counsellors should all be interpreted as forms of religious/spiritual counselling unless the policy clearly indicates otherwise.
