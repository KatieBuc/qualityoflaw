3.1 | Integrated service unit | Is there an integrated service unit for women victims of violence (VAW)?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly establishes, recognises, or mandates an integrated service unit, one-stop service centre, integrated service centre, or equivalent mechanism specifically for women victims/survivors of violence;
- the policy identifies a dedicated unit or facility that coordinates multiple services for VAW victims, such as health services, legal assistance, psychosocial support, shelter, protection, police support, referral services, or case management;
- the policy assigns responsibility to an existing integrated service provider or centre for receiving, assisting, protecting, or referring women victims of violence;
- the policy refers to recognised VAW service institutions or mechanisms (for example, P2TP2A, UPTD PPA, women and child protection service units, or equivalent) as providing integrated services for victims of violence.
Code No if:
- the policy only mentions general women's empowerment programs, training, education, employment support, economic assistance, or social welfare services without establishing or referring to an integrated VAW service mechanism;
- the policy refers only to coordination, cooperation, advocacy, socialisation, or general service provision without identifying a dedicated integrated service unit for VAW victims;
- no integrated service unit or equivalent VAW victim support mechanism is mentioned anywhere in the policy.
Do not count general women's empowerment activities (such as skills training, job training, capital assistance, business support, or economic empowerment programs) as an integrated service unit. The indicator requires a dedicated mechanism for responding to violence against women, not general support services.
3.2 | Police specialised service unit | Is there a specialised service unit for victims of violence against women (VAW) within police offices?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly establishes, recognises, or refers to a specialised police unit responsible for assisting victims of VAW;
- the policy mentions police-based specialised units for women and children victims of violence, including Women and Children Service Units (UPPA), Women and Children Protection Units, RPK (Ruang Pelayanan Khusus / Special Service Room), or equivalent police victim service units;
- the policy identifies a police office/unit that provides specialised services for VAW victims, such as receiving reports, providing protection, referrals, assistance, or coordination with other services;
- the policy states that integrated service units for women and children victims of violence operate through or are located within police structures, including UPPA or equivalent police units.
Code No if:
- the policy only mentions police generally (for example, police involvement in enforcement, investigation, or coordination) without identifying a specialised victim service unit;
- the policy refers only to general integrated service centres, hospitals, shelters, NGOs, or other non-police services without a police-based specialised unit;
- no specialised police unit for VAW victims is mentioned anywhere in the policy.
Do not require the policy to use the exact phrase "specialised service unit". References to recognised police victim support units (such as UPPA or RPK) are sufficient for a Yes. General police responsibilities alone are not sufficient.
3.3 | Reporting/hotline service | Does the policy provide a mechanism or service for receiving reports or complaints of violence?
Code Yes if the policy includes:
complaint or reporting services;
a complaint, reporting, or hotline mechanism;
procedures or SOPs for receiving complaints;
a service unit responsible for receiving reports or complaints.
A dedicated hotline or specialised reporting unit is not required.
Code No if the policy only refers to administrative reporting between government agencies or reporting obligations that do not involve receiving complaints from victims or the public.
3.4 | Safety/protection shelter | Is there any service unit that provides safety/protection shelter for victims of violence against women (VAW)?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly mentions safe houses, shelters, temporary shelters, protection shelters, crisis shelters, refuge centres, or equivalent accommodation-based protection for women victims of violence;
- the policy identifies a service unit, integrated service centre, crisis centre, or other mechanism that provides or includes shelter/safe accommodation for VAW victims;
- the policy assigns responsibility for providing temporary residence, emergency accommodation, protection housing, or a safe place for women victims of violence;
- the policy refers to recognised shelter services for victims of violence, including shelters operated by government agencies, social services, hospitals, NGOs, or integrated service providers.
Code No if:
- the policy only mentions general protection, assistance, rights, housing, or welfare support without referring to shelter, safe accommodation, or a refuge service for VAW victims;
- the policy mentions service units but does not include any shelter, safe house, temporary accommodation, or protection residence function;
- no shelter or accommodation-based protection mechanism is mentioned anywhere in the policy.
Do not require the policy to establish a standalone shelter unit. A reference to a safe house, shelter, or equivalent protection accommodation within an integrated service unit or service framework is sufficient for a Yes.

3.7 | Psychosocial counselling | Does the policy include psychosocial or psychological counselling services for victims?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
psychosocial counselling, psychological counselling, psychological services, psychological assistance, counselling, therapy, trauma counselling, trauma recovery, or psychosocial support is provided;
psychological or psychosocial services are included within victim services, rehabilitation, recovery, or assistance;
psychological professionals (e.g., psychologists, counsellors) are identified as service providers.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
general rehabilitation or recovery without psychological or psychosocial support;
psychological harm or trauma without describing a counselling or support service;
mental health as a principle or objective without a corresponding service.
3.8 | Religious/spiritual counselling | Does the policy include religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, mentoring, or similar faith-based psychosocial support as a service available to victims?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
religious or spiritual counselling is explicitly listed as a service;
spiritual guidance, religious guidance, spiritual mentoring, religious mentoring, pastoral care, or similar faith-based support is provided to victims;
services are delivered by religious leaders, spiritual counsellors, clerics, chaplains, religious mentors, or similar personnel;
victim services are implemented by institutions or agencies responsible for religious, faith and spiritual affairs;
religious or spiritual support forms part of rehabilitation, recovery, counselling, or integrated victim services.
A dedicated or specialised service unit is not required. Any explicit provision of religious or spiritual counselling, guidance, or support is sufficient.
Code No if the policy only:
mentions religion or spirituality as a general principle;
refers to religious organisations without describing counselling or support services;
refers to "mental and spiritual" matters without indicating the provision of support or services.
For this indicator, "spiritual guidance", "religious guidance", "spiritual services", "mental and spiritual services", and services provided by religious or spiritual counsellors should all be interpreted as forms of religious/spiritual counselling unless the policy clearly indicates otherwise.
3.9 | Resocialisation services | Is there any service unit that provides resocialisation (rehabilitation/reintegration) services?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy identifies a service unit responsible for rehabilitation, resocialisation, reintegration, recovery, or similar services for VAW victims;
- the policy states that an integrated service unit provides or is responsible for rehabilitation/reintegration services;
- the policy mentions rehabilitation or social reintegration services within the scope of victim support services.
Code No if:
- rehabilitation/reintegration is mentioned only as a general concept without linking it to a service unit or victim support mechanism;
- no service unit or responsible institution is identified as providing these services.
Do not require a standalone resocialisation unit. If the policy assigns rehabilitation or reintegration services to an integrated service unit, this is sufficient for Yes.
