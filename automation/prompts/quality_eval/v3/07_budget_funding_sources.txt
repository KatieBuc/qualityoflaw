7.3 | International support/donors | Is there any funding, budget source, or other explicit international support for women’s protection/VAW measures, including international organizations, foreign governments, bilateral or multilateral donors, UN agencies, development partners, or other international assistance?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly mentions international assistance, international support, donor support, foreign aid, development partners, international organizations, UN agencies, bilateral or multilateral cooperation, or equivalent international resources related to women's protection, VAW services, gender equality, or implementation of the policy;
- the policy identifies international organizations, donors, or external partners as participants, contributors, supporters, coordinators, or sources of assistance for women's protection or related programs;
- the policy includes international funding, grants, assistance, cooperation, or partnership arrangements in implementation, monitoring, service delivery, capacity building, or institutional support provisions;
- the policy cites international institutions or mechanisms as part of the legal foundation, implementation framework, or policy support structure where this indicates international involvement.
Code No if:
- the policy only mentions domestic government budgets, local funding, community participation, businesses, NGOs, or general institutional cooperation without any international component;
- the policy mentions international standards, conventions, or general references to global principles but does not indicate international support, assistance, funding, partnerships, or involvement;
- no explicit international support, donor, organization, or assistance source is mentioned anywhere in the policy.
Do not require a specific budget amount, funding allocation, or named donor. An explicit reference to international assistance, international support, or international institutional participation related to women's protection is sufficient for a Yes.
