2.1 | Institutional network | Does the policy establish an institutional coordination mechanism or network to support the implementation of prevention, protection, or response to violence against women and children?
Code Yes if the policy includes any formal coordination or cooperation mechanism, including:
coordination or cooperation between government agencies or departments;
cross-sectoral or multi-agency coordination;
institutional networks, integrated service networks, referral networks, task forces, committees, or coordination forums;
coordination between different administrative levels (e.g. provincial, district/city, sub-district, village);
coordination or cooperation between different jurisdictions;
coordination with other public institutions where they form part of the policy's implementation mechanism.
Cross-jurisdictional coordination is not required. Coordination within a jurisdiction or between government sectors is sufficient.
Code No if the policy merely assigns responsibilities to individual agencies without establishing coordination, cooperation, or a network between them.
2.2 | Collaboration with NGOs | Is there any collaboration/cooperation mechanism with non-government service providers (e.g., NGOs, community organisations, civil society groups)?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly mentions cooperation, coordination, partnership, or collaboration with NGOs, civil society organisations, community organisations, or non-government service providers;
- the policy identifies NGOs or non-government actors as partners in prevention, protection, services, referrals, advocacy, implementation, or monitoring;
- the policy establishes a forum, network, working group, or coordination mechanism involving NGOs or non-government organisations.
Code No if:
- the policy only mentions government agencies, businesses, or general community participation without identifying non-government organisations/service providers;
- no collaboration mechanism with NGOs or non-government service providers is mentioned.
Do not require a formal forum or working group. Any explicit partnership or cooperation arrangement with NGOs or non-government service providers is sufficient for Yes.
2.3 | Data collection systems | Does the policy establish, require, or assign responsibility for a system to collect, document, record, monitor, report, manage, exchange, or maintain data/information on violence against women, victims, cases, or women's protection? Answer yes or no.
Code Yes if the policy:
establishes or mandates a VAW/women's protection data or information system;
requires the collection, recording, documentation, monitoring, reporting, management, presentation, or exchange of data/information on victims or cases;
assigns an agency responsibility for maintaining, developing, or managing VAW-related data or information.
Code No if the policy only:
gives victims a right to receive information;
establishes referral or communication networks without a data/information function;
requires ordinary administrative reports that do not involve collecting or managing VAW case data.
2.4 | Gender mainstreaming institutionalisation | Does the policy establish or require gender mainstreaming as an institutional mechanism for implementing women's protection or VAW policies and programs?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
gender mainstreaming is explicitly required or implemented as part of prevention, protection, planning, implementation, or service delivery;
the policy establishes or refers to a gender mainstreaming working group, focal point, institution, coordination mechanism, or institutional arrangement;
gender mainstreaming is integrated into government policies, programs, planning, budgeting, or institutional responsibilities;
institutional strengthening or coordination is explicitly for implementing gender mainstreaming.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
gender equality, gender justice, or gender-responsive budgeting without mentioning gender mainstreaming;
general institutional strengthening unrelated to gender mainstreaming;
women's empowerment or gender-related objectives without establishing or requiring gender mainstreaming as an implementation mechanism.
2.5 | Training and capacity building | Does the policy require or provide training, technical guidance, institutional capacity building, or professional development for government officials, implementing agencies, service providers, or other personnel responsible for implementing this policy?
Code Yes if the policy includes:
training for government officials, public servants, or implementing agencies;
training for health workers, police, social workers, legal personnel, counsellors, or other service providers;
technical guidance, operational guidance, professional development, or institutional strengthening for implementing personnel;
capacity building or strengthening of government institutions, service providers, or implementation mechanisms;
guidance or training provided by government to organisations responsible for delivering services under the policy.
Do not code Yes solely because:
services are provided by "trained officers" or "trained personnel";
staff qualifications or competency requirements are described;
victims receive job training, entrepreneurship training, livelihood training, political education, or other empowerment activities;
community members receive awareness raising, advocacy, parenting, or prevention training;
the policy refers generally to "capacity building" without indicating that it applies to government institutions, implementing agencies, or service providers.
Code Yes only where the policy requires or provides capacity building for those responsible for implementing, coordinating, or delivering services under the policy.
