2.1 | Institutional network | Does the policy establish an institutional coordination mechanism or network to support the implementation of prevention, protection, or response to violence against women and children?
Code Yes if the policy includes any formal coordination or cooperation mechanism, including:
coordination or cooperation between government agencies or departments;
cross-sectoral or multi-agency coordination;
institutional networks, integrated service networks, referral networks, task forces, committees, or coordination forums;
coordination between different administrative levels (e.g. provincial, district/city, sub-district, village);
coordination or cooperation between different jurisdictions;
coordination with other public institutions where they form part of the policy's implementation mechanism.
Cross-jurisdictional coordination is not required. Coordination within a jurisdiction or between government sectors is sufficient.
Code No if the policy merely assigns responsibilities to individual agencies without establishing coordination, cooperation, or a network between them.

2.4 | Gender mainstreaming institutionalisation | is there any specific mention of gender mainstreaming institutionalisation or working group for gender mainstreaming to deliver VAW policies and programs? yes or no
2.5 | Training and capacity building | Does the policy require or provide training, technical guidance, institutional capacity building, or professional development for government officials, implementing agencies, service providers, or other personnel responsible for implementing this policy?
Code Yes if the policy includes:
training for government officials, public servants, or implementing agencies;
training for health workers, police, social workers, legal personnel, counsellors, or other service providers;
technical guidance, operational guidance, professional development, or institutional strengthening for implementing personnel;
capacity building or strengthening of government institutions, service providers, or implementation mechanisms;
guidance or training provided by government to organisations responsible for delivering services under the policy.
Do not code Yes solely because:
services are provided by "trained officers" or "trained personnel";
staff qualifications or competency requirements are described;
victims receive job training, entrepreneurship training, livelihood training, political education, or other empowerment activities;
community members receive awareness raising, advocacy, parenting, or prevention training;
the policy refers generally to "capacity building" without indicating that it applies to government institutions, implementing agencies, or service providers.
Code Yes only where the policy requires or provides capacity building for those responsible for implementing, coordinating, or delivering services under the policy.
