4.1 | Information and education about VAW | Does the policy include information, awareness, socialization, communication, or education activities specifically to prevent violence against women or protect women from violence?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
information, education, awareness-raising, public communication, campaigns, counseling, or socialization are explicitly described as measures to prevent violence against women;
education or information is provided on violence against women, women's rights, protection from violence, or relevant laws and regulations as part of prevention;
the policy includes community education, dissemination of information, or public awareness activities aimed at preventing violence against women;
information and education activities are listed as prevention programs, prevention strategies, or prevention responsibilities.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
education or information unrelated to preventing violence against women;
education as a general right or service;
women's rights or legal rights without an associated information, awareness, education, or prevention activity;
general socialization or education that is not linked to preventing violence against women or protecting women from violence.
4.2 | Gender equality education | Does the policy include primary prevention activities that provide information, education, awareness raising, communication, socialisation, campaigns, training, advocacy or similar educational activities specifically about gender equality and/or women's rights?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly includes prevention activities that educate or raise awareness about:
gender equality;
equal rights of women and men;
women's rights;
non-discrimination against women;
gender-sensitive attitudes, norms or behaviours.
Such activities may include communication, information, education (CIE/KIE), public awareness campaigns, school or community education, training, advocacy or socialisation.
Do not code Yes solely because the policy:
defines gender equality or women's rights;
lists gender equality or women's rights as guiding principles, objectives or policy aims;
states that services should be gender-responsive;
includes legal definitions or explanatory text;
generally promotes women's empowerment without an educational or awareness-raising component.
Code No if the policy only recognises gender equality or women's rights without requiring education or awareness activities.

4.4 | Prevention through education | Does the policy include education as an explicit strategy to prevent violence against women or children?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly identifies education as a prevention measure, including:
education to prevent violence against women or children;
educational programmes, school-based initiatives, or curriculum designed to prevent violence;
critical education, public education, or awareness education that is explicitly intended to reduce or prevent violence;
improving access to, quality of, or content of education where this is explicitly linked to preventing violence or reducing the risk of violence;
education or training described within the policy's prevention strategy or prevention chapter.
Do not code Yes solely because the policy:
expands access to education;
establishes schools, educational institutions, scholarships, or compulsory education;
provides formal or non-formal education;
provides vocational, skills, employment, entrepreneurship, or political education;
promotes women's empowerment through education;
generally improves education or educational quality without explicitly linking these measures to the prevention of violence.
Code Yes only when education is explicitly presented as a prevention mechanism for violence.
Do not infer that education prevents violence. The policy must explicitly identify education as a prevention measure or include it within a prevention strategy.
