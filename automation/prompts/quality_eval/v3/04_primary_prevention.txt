4.1 | Information and education about VAW | Does the policy include information, awareness, socialisation, communication, or education activities specifically about violence against women or anti-violence values as a preventive measure?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
information, education, critical education, awareness-raising, public communication, campaigns, counseling, or socialization are explicitly described to provide education or information about violence against women, gender-based violence, protection from violence, dangers of violence, prohibition of violence, protection of women from violence, or anti-violence values or norms;
the policy includes community education, dissemination of information, or public awareness activities that are explicitly described to provide education or information about violence against women, gender-based violence, protection from violence, dangers of violence, prohibition of violence, or anti-violence values or norms;
these information and education activities are listed as prevention programs, prevention strategies, or prevention responsibilities.
Code No if the policy refers only to:
education or information unrelated to preventing violence against women;
education, awareness or information about women’s rights, victims’ rights, or gender equality;
education, awareness or information about laws, regulations, or policies related to violence against women;
education as a general right or service;
women's rights or legal rights without an associated information, awareness, education, or prevention activity;
general socialization or education that is not linked to preventing violence against women or protecting women from violence.
4.2 | Gender equality education | Does the policy include primary prevention activities that provide information, education, awareness raising, communication, socialisation, campaigns, training, advocacy or similar educational activities specifically about gender equality and/or women's rights?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly includes prevention activities that educate or raise awareness about:
gender equality or gender justice;
equal rights of women and men;
women's rights or protection of women’s rights;
victim’s rights;
non-discrimination against women or eliminating discrimination against women;
gender-sensitive or gender-responsive attitudes, norms or behaviours.
Such activities may include communication, information, education (CIE/KIE), critical education, public awareness campaigns, school or community education, training, advocacy or socialisation;
Such activities must be explicitly situated as a strategy or measure to prevent violence against women.
Do not code Yes solely because the policy:
defines gender equality or women's rights;
lists gender equality or women's rights as guiding principles, objectives or policy aims;
states that services should be gender-responsive;
includes legal definitions or explanatory text;
generally promotes women's empowerment without an educational or awareness-raising component;
education, awareness or information about violence against women, gender-based violence, protection from violence, dangers of violence, prohibition of violence, protection of women from violence, or anti-violence values or norms;
education, awareness or information about laws, regulations, or policies related to violence against women;
education, training, advocacy, or capacity building programs about gender mainstreaming;
mentions only information and education activities for children and only for preventing violence against children.
Code No if the policy only recognises gender equality or women's rights without requiring education or awareness activities.
4.3 | VAW laws awareness | Is there any primary prevention activity providing information or education about violence against women (VAW) laws and policies?
Code Yes if any of the following apply:
- the policy explicitly mentions socialisation, dissemination, education, awareness-raising, or information activities about laws, regulations, or policies related to violence against women, domestic violence, sexual violence, human trafficking, or child marriage;
- the policy explicitly mentions public education on legal awareness or legal protections related to violence against women and protection of victims of violence;
- the policy explicitly mentions awareness activities targeting communities, institutions, or service providers about VAW laws, regulations, or policies.
Such activities must be explicitly situated as a strategy or measure to prevent violence against women.
Code No if:
- the policy only provides services or responses after violence occurs without any information/education component;
- awareness activities are mentioned but are unrelated to VAW laws, regulations, or policies.
Do not require the exact phrase "VAW laws awareness". Socialisation, education activities, or dissemination of laws and regulations related to protection from violence against women is sufficient for Yes.
Do not infer that the policy includes general legal awareness-raising through communication, information and education services. The policy must specify that the information or education is about laws, regulations or policies on violence against women.
4.4 | Prevention through education | Does the policy include education as an explicit strategy to prevent violence against women or children?
Code Yes if the policy explicitly identifies involvement of education institutions or improvements of education, educational curricula, or quality of education as a preventive measure, including:
educational programmes, school-based initiatives, or curriculum designed to prevent violence against women;
improving access to, quality of, or content of education where this is explicitly linked to preventing violence or reducing the risk of violence;
improving educational standards or programs as a preventive measure to protect women from violence;
providing education materials or media related to women’s rights or violence against women as a preventive measure;
providing education for victims of violence as a preventive measure;
prevention initiatives delivered by or involving education field or education institutions, including government organisations or apparatus that are responsible for managing education field or activities;
incorporating teaching and learning materials related to women’s rights and violence against women as a measure to prevent violence,
developing schools or education institutions, including learning or training centres, that provide educational activities or capacity building to prevent violence against women.
Do not code Yes solely because the policy:
expands access to education;
only identifies education measures specifically targeting children as a strategy to prevent violence against children;
mentions education activities in broad terms, such as socialisation, critical education, training, capacity building, or awareness education without explicitly identifying a more formal form of education instruments, such as education material or media, teaching and learning materials, education curricula, school, or learning and training centres, as a measure to prevent violence;
mentions education activities in broad terms without explicit involvement of education institutions, such as schools, universities, learning or training centres, or government organisations or apparatus responsible for education;
establishes schools, educational institutions, scholarships, or compulsory education;
provides formal or non-formal education;
provides vocational, skills, employment, entrepreneurship, or political education;
promotes women's empowerment through education;
generally improves education or educational quality without explicitly linking these measures to the prevention of violence;
Code Yes only when education is explicitly presented as a prevention mechanism for violence.
Do not infer that education prevents violence. The policy must explicitly identify education as a prevention measure or include it within a prevention strategy.
